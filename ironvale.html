Ironvale Skirmish

Two banners, one valley, four ages. Put villagers on the berries and the forest, raise camps to shorten their walk, climb from the Dark Age to Imperial, and field the army that beats whatever the crimson host brings.

Most of the gold lies away from home. Scout it, plant camps on it, and hold the ground between you and the crimson host — on the largest map there is five times the territory to claim.

The Chronicle down the right sets sixteen tasks and pays a bounty for each. Your progress saves — three slots and an autosave — and you can export a save file to keep.
